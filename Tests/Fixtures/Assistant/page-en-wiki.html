Seoul

Seoul, officially Seoul Special Metropolitan City, is the capital and largest city of South Korea. As of 2024 the city proper had a registered population of 9,384,000 people, down from a peak of over ten million in the early 1990s.

The Seoul Capital Area, which includes Incheon and most of Gyeonggi Province, is home to roughly 26 million residents — about half of the national population & one of the largest metropolitan areas in the world.

Geography

The city sits on the Han River, which divides it into the northern and southern halves. Bukhansan National Park rises to 836 metres on the northern edge of the city limits.
